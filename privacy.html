Electric City Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Electric City is a game that teaches how electricity works. It is made for children and grown-ups.

What we collect

Nothing. Electric City does not collect, store or share any personal information about anyone who plays it.

  • No accounts, sign-ins, names, email addresses or ages are asked for.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party code that collects data.
  • The game works fully offline. It does not connect to the internet.
  • There are no chats, no sharing and no in-app purchases.

What stays on the device

The game saves your progress (levels finished, stars, sparks, words learned and what you built) on your own device, so you can carry on where you left off. This never leaves the device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

The "Read it to me" button uses the device's built-in voice. The words are read aloud by the device itself and are not sent anywhere.

Children

Electric City is designed for children and follows Apple's rules for the Kids Category. Because we collect no information at all, there is nothing for a parent to review or delete. Grown-up settings sit behind a grown-up check.

Changes
